## Purpose
- This section acts as a mock banking backend. Since real banks (fortunately) do not expose their production schemas, these tables are AI-generated approximations created with Claude to simulate realistic banking data. While all future updates and refinements are purely human-made.

## Development Status
- The schema is under active iteration and is prone to breaking changes. While `oumi` is currently scoped as a mock bank, the project's direction may pivot entirely as new ideas develop.
